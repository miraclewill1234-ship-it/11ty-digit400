Digital innovation refers to the development and use of technology to improve processes, introduce new ideas, and create new opportunities.

In creative industries, innovation can be seen through graphic design software, digital marketing, virtual experiences, website development, and social media platforms.

These technologies allow individuals and businesses to experiment with new ways of creating and sharing their work.

### How Is Technology Changing Creativity?

- Digital design tools make creating graphics and visual content easier.
- Social media allows creators to promote their work.
- Virtual experiences introduce new ways to engage audiences.
- Digital platforms help businesses connect with customers.

### The Benefits and Challenges

While digital innovation creates opportunities, it also requires individuals to continuously learn new tools and adapt to changing technology. Not everyone has equal access to the resources and skills needed to participate.
